### Detailed Candidate Profiles

#### Candidate 1: Anuvritta (Top Recommendation ★★★★★)
- **Pronunciation:** /ə.nuːˈvrɪt.tə/ (*uh-noo-VRIT-tuh*)
- **Etymology:** From Sanskrit **अनुवृत्त** (*Anuvṛtta*), formed by prefixing **Anu** (*following along, in continuous alignment with, in harmony*) to **Vritta** (*conduct, cadence, rhythm*).
- **Why It Is Brilliant for This Product:**
  - It carries the exact same linguistic and spiritual DNA as **VRITTA**, but elevates it: it literally means **"remaining in continuous rhythm; abiding in mindful cadence without friction"**.
  - **Domain Verification:** `https://anuvritta.vercel.app` is **verified 100% available** (returns `HTTP 404 DEPLOYMENT_NOT_FOUND`). You can claim the pure, unhyphenated name directly on Vercel!
  - **Zero Market Collisions:** Our global search confirmed there are zero competing habit trackers, to-do apps, or productivity tools named "Anuvritta".
- **Drawbacks:** 4 syllables (though phonetically melodic and rhythmic).

---

#### Candidate 2: Swadhyay (or Swadhyaya) (★★★★☆)
- **Pronunciation:** /svɑːdˈhjɑːj/ (*swahd-HYAY*)
- **Etymology:** From Sanskrit **स्वाध्याय** (*Svādhyāya*), one of the five *Niyamas* in classical yoga, meaning **"intentional self-study, reflection upon noble literature, and contemplation of one's own mind"**.
- **Why It Is Brilliant for This Product:**
  - It describes the actual daily habits with extraordinary literal accuracy:
    - Norman Lewis *Word Power Made Easy* (daily self-study).
    - 30 Osho discourses (listening and self-inquiry).
    - Bedtime reading (literature).
    - Private diary (self-reflection and examining assumptions).
  - **Domain Verification:** `https://swadhyay.vercel.app` is **verified 100% available** on Vercel (`HTTP 404`).
- **Drawbacks:** Very strong philosophical/academic connotation; slightly less focused on the physical exercise aspect.

---

#### Candidate 3: Charyam (★★★★☆)
- **Pronunciation:** /ˈtʃɑːr.jʌm/ (*CHAR-yum*)
- **Etymology:** From Sanskrit **चर्यम्** (*Caryam*), denoting **"that which is to be practiced daily; noble conduct, daily discipline, and the mindful path of life"**.
- **Why It Is Brilliant for This Product:**
  - Clean, architectural 2-syllable word.
  - Root of *Dinacharya* (daily routine) and *Ritucharya* (seasonal balance).
  - **Domain Verification:** `https://charyam.vercel.app` is **verified available** (`HTTP 404`).
- **Drawbacks:** Some users may ask whether it's pronounced with an English "ch" or Sanskrit soft palatal.

---

#### Candidate 4: Samyama (★★★★☆)
- **Pronunciation:** /ˈsʌm.jə.mə/ (*SUM-yuh-muh*)
- **Etymology:** From Sanskrit **संयम** (*Saṃyama*), Patanjali's culmination of concentration (*Dharana*), meditation (*Dhyana*), and absorption (*Samadhi*).
- **Why It Fits:** Represents the balanced integration of physical movement, breath, and intellectual focus.
- **Domain Verification:** `https://samyama.vercel.app` is **verified available** (`HTTP 404`).

---

#### Candidate 5: Sahaja (★★★★☆)
- **Pronunciation:** /səˈhʌ.dʒə/ (*suh-HUH-juh*)
- **Etymology:** From Sanskrit **सहज** (*Sahaja*), meaning **"natural, effortless, inborn, spontaneous flow"**.
- **Why It Fits:** Directly mirrors Osho's core teaching and the companion's non-punitive philosophy: consistency without unnatural strain, guilt, or rigid deadlines.
- **Subdomain:** `sahaja-routine.vercel.app` (verified 404).
